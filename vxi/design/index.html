Design Outlet

DESIGN & ARCHITECTURE · 10 levels · 47 studios

Walk with W A S D or the arrow keys, hold Shift to run, drag to look, and press Enter to go to what the reticle rests on. Escape steps outside. Inside, M opens the visor's tactical map, a map of the whole stack where arrow keys choose a studio and Enter goes there, and V takes the visor off or puts it on. A narrator, an AI voice, reads aloud the display you stand at or look at, with captions; N turns it off or on. The directory below the view lists every level and studio.

Visit the Design Outlet

10 glass levels, one for each designer, in the order of their births: Filippo Brunelleschi at street level, Zaha Hadid at the top. Each of the 47 studios is a small museum of one work or one idea: a stele, its hologram on a dais, a lesson, where it leads, and a side room where the hologram builds itself. Some units stand vacant, between tenants.
