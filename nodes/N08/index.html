01Definition

!Unverified

Metabolism is the full set of chemical reactions that break nutrients down for energy (catabolism) and build the molecules the body needs (anabolism).

05Functions4

  • !Unverified

    Extracts energy from carbohydrates, fats and proteins.

  • !Unverified

    Builds and renews tissues, enzymes and other molecules.

  • !Unverified

    Shifts fuel use between fed and fasted states.

  • !Unverified

    Converts waste products for clearance, largely through the liver and kidneys.

07Relationships6

  • N03MitochondriaT01suppliesMetabolismR005

    !Unverified

    Mitochondria carry out the citric acid cycle and fatty-acid oxidation, central steps of energy metabolism.

  • N06EnergyT02depends onMetabolismR010

    !Unverified

    Usable energy comes from metabolizing carbohydrates, fats and proteins.

  • N10AdaptationT02modulatesMetabolismR020

    !Unverified

    Regular training changes how muscles take up and use fuel, including glucose.

  • N15HormonalT03regulatesMetabolismR022

    !Unverified

    Hormones such as insulin, glucagon, thyroid hormone and cortisol set the pace and direction of metabolism.

  • N13InflammationT03modulatesMetabolismR027

    !Unverified

    Persistent low-grade inflammation is associated with disturbed metabolic regulation, such as reduced insulin sensitivity.

  • N18LiverT04regulatesMetabolismR043

    !Unverified

    The liver stores and releases glucose and processes fats and proteins.
