Evidence policy

Method

How TRIARC labels what it knows. Every statement says whether it was checked against a source, is a framework construct, or is still unverified, so nothing reads as more certain than it is.

01Claim statuses

Sourced
Checked against at least one listed reference that was opened and read when the statement was written.
Conceptual
A TRIARC framework construct, such as an operating-system mapping or a tier-level link. Conceptual by definition; never graded.
!Unverified
Textbook-level wording that has not yet been checked against a source. Most structural nodes are in this state.

Shape carries the status, not colour: solid with a check, dashed outline, or hollow with “!”.

02Evidence grades

Evidence grades and their meaning
EstablishedTextbook physiology or a current consensus guideline
SupportedConsistent systematic reviews or meta-analyses
EmergingLimited or mixed trials; observational or mechanistic evidence
InsufficientNot enough evidence to state
Not applicableTRIARC framework constructs, conceptual by definition

03Rules the build enforces

  1. Every statement carries a status, and every page shows it.
  2. A sourced statement cites at least one reference, and every cited reference exists in the source list with the date it was opened and checked.
  3. Conceptual statements carry no evidence grade.
  4. Numbers with clinical or dosing units (such as mmHg, mg/dL, bpm, IU, minutes or %) appear only in sourced statements.
  5. Outcome language such as cures, detox, reverses aging or prevents disease appears only in sourced statements graded established or supported.
  6. Full-depth nodes fill every section, state an overall grade, cite sources, and link at least one NUÏTRIX and one MUÏTRIX factor.
  7. A node is marked reviewed only with a named reviewer and a review date.

The number and outcome-language checks are pattern-based: they catch common forms but cannot prove absence. Human review remains the final check.

04Review

A node is marked reviewed only when a named, qualified person has checked it and dated the review. AI authoring never sets this status. No node has been reviewed yet; a qualified reviewer is required before any public release.

06Known overlaps and gaps

The canonical registry of 34 nodes is used exactly as issued. Where entries overlap or the body is not yet covered, TRIARC shows the issue instead of changing the data; a separate revision proposal handles fixes.

Overlaps

  • N10AdaptationT02N33AdaptationT07

    One name, two IDs.

    In V1: Scope notes: N10 is tissue and metabolic adaptation to load and stress; N33 is whole-system adaptive capacity. Every display shows ID and tier.

    Proposed: Rename N33 Adaptive Capacity, or fold it into N32 Resilience.

  • N11EndocrineT03N15HormonalT03

    The endocrine system is the hormone system: near-synonyms.

    In V1: N11 covers glands and axes; N15 covers hormonal signaling (messengers, receptors, feedback).

    Proposed: Merge into Endocrine & Hormonal Signaling, freeing a slot for a gap.

  • N25VisionT05N30SensesT06

    Vision is a sense, split across two tiers.

    In V1: N25 is the visual system including visual processing; N30 is the other senses.

    Proposed: Rename N30 Sensory Systems with vision inside it, or rename N25 Visual Processing.

  • N05HomeostasisT01

    Mostly a whole-body regulatory property; Tier 01 fits only cellular homeostasis.

    In V1: Scope note: cellular homeostasis (ion gradients, pH, protein quality), linked to N34.

    Proposed: Rename Cellular Homeostasis, or move to Tier 07.

  • N13InflammationT03N12ImmuneT03

    Inflammation is a process within the immune response.

    In V1: N13 is typed as a process and linked as part of N12.

    Proposed: Keep: it anchors the PROTECTION and RECOVERY lenses.
