How to read the stack

BIÖLOGIX stacks the body in seven tiers, from Tier 01 at the base (cells and molecules) to Tier 07 at the top (whole-system outcomes). Select a tier to see its nodes, then select a node for its research summary. The tiers group the registry; dependency between nodes is shown only by explicit relationships.

Drag to turn the stack; scroll or pinch to zoom.
